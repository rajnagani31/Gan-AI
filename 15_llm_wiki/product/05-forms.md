# Forms Page

Forms collect structured information from customers or internal teams. Open **Forms** in the sidebar and select **Create form** to start from a blank form or a template.

## Building a form

Add short text, long text, email, dropdown, checkbox, date, or file-upload fields. Turn on **Required** for questions respondents must answer. Use Preview to test the form before publishing.

## Publishing and submissions

After selecting **Publish**, copy the share link or embed code. Responses appear in the Submissions tab. Authorized users can filter results, search for a response, export submissions, and mark a submission as reviewed.
