# Product Overview

Acme Workspace is a team-management product for organizing projects, customer information, and internal requests. Users sign in to a workspace and see only the information their role allows.

## Main navigation

The left sidebar contains Dashboard, Projects, Customers, Forms, Reports, and Settings. The workspace switcher is at the top of the sidebar. The search button finds projects, customers, and form submissions.

## User roles

- Owner: manages billing, security, and all workspace settings.
- Admin: manages members, projects, and shared settings.
- Member: can work with permitted projects and records.
- Viewer: can view shared information but cannot edit it.
