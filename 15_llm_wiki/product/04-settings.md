# Settings Page

The Settings page controls workspace-wide configuration. It is available to Owners and Admins, although some sections are Owner-only.

## General settings

Change the workspace name, company logo, default language, and time zone. These choices affect the entire workspace.

## Members and access

Use **Members** to invite users by email, change their roles, or remove their access. Removing a member immediately prevents them from opening the workspace.

## Notifications and integrations

Workspace notification rules determine which alerts are sent for events such as new form submissions. The Integrations section connects external services and shows which permissions each connection has.

## Billing

Only the Owner can open Billing. This section shows the current plan, payment method, invoices, and renewal date.
